Governance

Motion is part of meaning, not polish.

Allowed

Functional motionMicro, spatial, event-driven operational, intelligence, real progress and gestures.
Causal deltaAnimate only values changed by a real event.

Not allowed

Arbitrary state cyclingNo generic “Next state” demos or production behavior.
Ambient AI motionNo glow, pulse, shimmer or sparkle loops.
Fake progressNo motion implying work not backed by system state.
