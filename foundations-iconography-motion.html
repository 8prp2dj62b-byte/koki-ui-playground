No mockup inheritance

Icons explain. They do not decorate.

KOKI iconography се създава като независим system language. Нито една икона, форма или treatment не се взима от съществуващите mockups. Замразените foundations определят цвят, spacing, radius и touch geometry; този слой добавя само icon grammar.

KOKI Motion Language

Motion explains what changed, where it moved and who is acting.

KOKI няма една универсална анимация. Motion е система от пет функционални нива плюс gesture behavior. Всяко движение трябва да предава state, пространствена връзка, реален процес или промяна на control ownership.

Level 1 · Micro-interactions

Immediate, restrained acknowledgement.

Press, selection, focus, switch, checkbox and local state transitions confirm that input was received. No bounce, elastic overshoot or attention-seeking animation.

Press120ms acknowledgement. Movement maximum 1px where appropriate.
Local state180ms. Color, indicator position or visibility changes together.
FocusVisible immediately; never delayed by decorative transition.
Level 2 · Navigation & spatial continuity

The interface preserves origin and destination.

Detail views, sheets and back navigation use spatial motion so the user understands where the new context came from. Motion never changes information hierarchy; it only preserves continuity.

Level 3 · Operational state motion

State changes are visible without becoming theatrical.

When a conversation, listing or task changes from Waiting to Active, Attention or Completed, KOKI makes the transition legible with a brief coordinated state update.

Level 4 · KOKI Intelligence motion

KOKI has a motion signature for reasoning and control.

AI motion is structural, not decorative. The signature is a staged reveal: KOKI ownership appears through the Signal Rail, then Reason → Evidence → Confidence. Control handoff is shown as a directional transfer between KOKI and Human.

Gesture motion

Direct manipulation follows the finger.

Swipe, drag, sheet movement, dismiss and pull actions are not canned animations. During direct manipulation the object tracks input; motion tokens govern only release, settle, complete or cancel.

Governance

Motion is part of meaning, not polish.

After freeze, components may consume icon and motion tokens but cannot create their own visual animation language. New motion is allowed only when it communicates a new semantic relationship not covered by this foundation.

Allowed

Compose16/20/24px glyphs inside frozen component geometry.
Functional motionMicro, spatial, operational, intelligence, real progress and gestures.
Sequence meaningUse ordered reveal only when order itself explains the process.
Reduced motionReplace movement with immediate state/visibility change while preserving meaning.

Not allowed

Mockup inheritanceNo old icon treatments, component animations or visual conventions.
Ambient AI motionNo glow, pulse, shimmer, orbit, sparkle or breathing loops.
Fake progressNo animation that implies work, confidence or progress not backed by system state.
Ad-hoc timingNo component-specific duration/easing without foundation proposal.
