# KOKI Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 9 September 2026**

KOKI is a marketplace assistant that connects ChatGPT to a user's KOKI account and supported third-party marketplaces. This policy explains how information is handled when KOKI is used through ChatGPT.

## Information KOKI processes

KOKI may process the minimum information needed for the feature a user requests, including:

- KOKI account identifiers and profile information;
- marketplace connection status and opaque marketplace identifiers;
- listing information, listing images, prices, attributes and source links;
- marketplace conversation messages and message images;
- search criteria and saved search state;
- sale drafts, pricing analysis and listing optimization proposals;
- notification and automation settings;
- technical security, audit and error information needed to operate and protect the service.

KOKI does not return marketplace passwords, cookies, access tokens, API keys or other authentication secrets to ChatGPT tool responses.

## Authentication

When a user connects KOKI to ChatGPT, sign-in happens on KOKI's authorization service. KOKI credentials are submitted to KOKI, not to the conversational model. ChatGPT receives authorization tokens needed to call KOKI on the user's behalf, subject to the permissions and controls shown by ChatGPT.

## How information is used

KOKI uses information to:

- show the user's listings, searches, conversations and activity;
- retrieve real source-backed marketplace or property-search results;
- manage user-requested marketplace conversations and negotiation automation;
- create, analyze, optimize and publish user-approved sale listings;
- provide notifications and account controls;
- prevent abuse, enforce ownership boundaries, debug failures and maintain audit records.

KOKI does not sell personal information.

## Third-party services

KOKI may send task-specific information to services required to perform a requested workflow, including supported marketplaces such as OLX and Kleinanzeigen, property source imot.bg, and configured AI processing providers such as Google Gemini for tasks including classification, analysis, translation or draft generation. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

KOKI only sends information that is reasonably necessary for the requested workflow. Marketplace credentials remain server-side and are not exposed in ChatGPT tool output.

## Automated actions

If a user enables KOKI automation, KOKI may send marketplace messages or perform other enabled actions according to the user's configured strategy and controls. Users can stop automation or take over a supported conversation. Important external actions may also require approval in ChatGPT depending on the user's app-permission settings.

## Retention

KOKI keeps account, listing, conversation, draft, audit and operational data for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain history and security, comply with applicable obligations, or resolve disputes. Temporary authorization codes are short-lived. Users may disconnect KOKI from ChatGPT at any time. Requests concerning account data or deletion can be made through the KOKI support page.

## Security

KOKI uses authenticated, tenant-scoped requests, ownership checks, encrypted HTTPS transport, server-side marketplace credentials, action auditing and retry/idempotency controls where applicable. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Children

KOKI is intended for adults who are able to enter marketplace transactions and manage marketplace accounts. It is not designed for children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when KOKI's features, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
