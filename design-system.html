Governance rules

01 · Content structure is invariantSELL, BUY, Profile, Messages и wizard-ите използват една и съща hierarchy, typography, status semantics и spacing.
02 · Platform adaptation is selectiveАдаптираме navigation, sheets, dialogs, toggles, gestures, back behavior, touch feedback и system typography metrics. Не променяме бизнес компонентите.
03 · KOKI-owned AI components stay KOKIAI Recommendation, Confidence, Evidence, Price Rating, Agent Activity и Human Intervention изглеждат като KOKI и на iOS, и на Android.
04 · Semantic color is strictBrand = AI/selected; green = active/completed; amber = waiting; red = attention/error; neutral = inactive. BUY и SELL нямат отделни декоративни цветове.
05 · Liquid Glass has hierarchyGlass е за app shell, floating navigation, overlays и primary surfaces. Nested operational content използва quieter solid/translucent surfaces.
